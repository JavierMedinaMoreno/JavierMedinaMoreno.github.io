A Network Model of R&D, Competition and Intellectual Property

Bachelor's Degree Final Project | Universitat Autònoma de Barcelona
Maple | Network Theory | Quantitative Microeconomics

Microeconomic network model evaluating firm innovation strategies between original R&D and product reverse-engineering under several market structures. Its findings support the notion that intensified market competition suppresses research expenditure by eroding ROI, while relaxed patent enforcement mitigates duplicative R&D costs and narrows disparities between competing firms.
